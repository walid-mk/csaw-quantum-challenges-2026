local time occurs **nine hours later** in US–Canada. An award announcement at
09:00 GST corresponds to 00:00 ET; the listed regional ceremonies are not one
simultaneous broadcast.

The QAML submission cutoff is one global instant: **14 November 2026 at 04:00 UTC**.
Submit a new issue with your exact commit SHA in your group's private repository
before this cutoff. Material corrections will be distributed to every group.
