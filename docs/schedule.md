GST is UTC+04:00. ET is EST (UTC−05:00) on 13–14 November 2026. Thus the same
local time occurs **nine hours later** in US–Canada. An award announcement at
09:00 GST corresponds to 00:00 ET; the listed regional ceremonies are not one
simultaneous broadcast. Organizers will publish explicit ISO 8601 opening and
closing timestamps before submissions open. “Full day” is not an invented
00:00–23:59 submission window.

Keep regional standings and access grants separate. Do not share solutions or
private feedback between regional editions while either edition remains open.

The organizer preparation sequence is: finalize the kit and author handoffs,
verify team access and the remote prediction service, rehearse submission and
grading, then announce the opening decision. Service outages and any approved
schedule adjustment will be recorded here consistently for affected teams.
