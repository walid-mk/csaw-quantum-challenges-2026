# Event schedule

Updated 11 November 2026. Start solving QAML as soon as the materials are released;
do not wait for the in-person challenge day.

| Milestone | MENA | US–Canada (online) |
|---|---|---|
| QAML materials and submissions open | 11 November 2026, from release | 11 November release; online participation |
| QAML final submission deadline | **Friday, 13 November, 6:00 PM GST** | **Friday, 13 November, 6:00 PM ET** |
| Challenge-day support | Friday, 13 November, in person | Friday, 13 November, online |
| Optional challenge-day hints | 13 November | 13 November, shared remotely |
| Winners announced | Saturday, 14 November, 9:00–10:00 AM GST, CSAW MENA Award Ceremony | Saturday, 14 November, 9:00–11:00 AM ET, CSAW US–Canada Award Ceremony |

GST is UTC+04:00. ET is EST (UTC−05:00) on these dates. The regional deadlines
are **different instants**: MENA closes at 14:00 UTC on 13 November; US–Canada
closes at 23:00 UTC on 13 November (03:00 GST on 14 November). These replace the
previous global cutoff. The earlier deadlines leave time to validate and test
submissions. Submit working versions early so access or packaging issues can be
resolved before your regional deadline.

Submit an issue containing the full commit SHA in your team's private repository.
Keep solutions and feedback private until both editions have finished. The
US–Canada QAML challenge is online; competition participation does not require
travel to Brooklyn. Remote ceremony arrangements, if available, will be announced
by the event organizers; a livestream is not assumed.
